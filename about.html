Transforming Urban Spaces Into High-Recall Landmarks

Khusboo Entertainment was founded with a singular ambition: to provide businesses and institutions in Chhattisgarh with commanding, high-impact outdoor media infrastructure that delivers measurable results.

From iconic high-rise unipoles towering over the Raipur-Bilaspur highway to crystal-clear 4K LED billboard screens at Jaistambh Chowk and Telibandha VIP Road, we turn daily commutes into premium visual experiences.

Over 15 years, we have proudly executed statewide awareness campaigns for the Government of Chhattisgarh (Chhattisgarh Samvad, Women & Child Development, Social Welfare) as well as premier private enterprises such as Nakoda Group, ASG Eye Hospitals, and prominent retail jewelers.
